# Bubie Pack

**A tech and magic kitchen-sink modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1, made to play with friends.**

*🇧🇷 Versão em português mais abaixo.*

Create, Mekanism, Applied Energistics 2, Ars Nouveau, Mystical Agriculture, Apotheosis and 200+ more mods, tuned to work well together: no duplicate ores, integrations between the power and storage systems, bigger villages, and a pack light enough to run on a modest PC.

## 🤖 New in 1.0.0: Bubie

![Bubies dressed up in the editor](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2241/432/01-lineup-png.png)

**Bubie**, the little robot from bubie.gg, is the third mod made for this pack. Give it a job and an area and it works on its own, then stores everything in your chest:

- **Lumberjack, Digger, Quarry, Farmer, Miner and Collector**, with the Bubie Clipboard to mark the area and the chest.
- **Builder**: hand it a **Base Blueprint** and it raises the base block by block from its chest. Set a Lumberjack or a Quarry filling the same chest and they work as a team.
- Built with the pack's own tech: crafted from **voltaic ingots**, its station runs on any FE cable, and a **Voltaic pocket accumulator** keeps it charged.
- **Edit my Bubie**: casing, hoodie and screen-light colours, eyes and mouth, like My Bubie on bubie.gg.
- A new quest chapter whose last quest gives you **a second Bubie**.

## Why Bubie Pack

**A balanced world**
- A single ore generator: Mekanism's duplicate veins are turned off and ATO (All the Ores) handles everything.
- Smaller, more numerous veins: you find ore everywhere, but you won't mine a whole chunk of osmium in one go.
- **GeOre** geodes for every vanilla ore, as in ATM11 (coal, copper, iron, gold, diamond, emerald, lapis, redstone, quartz and ancient debris). No dowsing rods: you find them by exploring.
- **Almost Unified**: one item per metal. No more two kinds of zinc or tin that don't stack.

**Made for playing together**
- **Simple Voice Chat**: proximity voice chat.
- **Comforts** (sleeping bags and hammocks that don't move your spawn) and **Cosmetic Armor Reworked** (show the look you want over your real armor).
- **FTB Teams, Chunks and Essentials**: teams, claim protection, chunk loading and `/home`.
- **Corail Tombstone**: your items are kept safe in a grave when you die.
- **Better Compatibility Checker**: the server list shows whether your pack version matches the server's.
- **Simple Backups**: automatic world backups every 2 hours (keeps the last 10).
- **Crash Assistant** (shows what crashed and which mod caused it, so friends can send you the log).

**Light and fast**
- Sodium, Lithium, ModernFix, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling and Clumps.
- **All The Leaks** (memory leak fixes), **Get It Together, Drops!** (dropped items merge, fewer entities), **Fast Workbench**, NetherPortalFix and Model Gap Fix.
- **AI Improvements** (lighter mob AI) and **Sodium Extra** (FPS counter, toggles for particles, animations, fog and more).
- **Euphoria Patches**: extra options for the included Complementary shaders, plus **Iris Search** to find shader settings.
- **Iris** with **Complementary Shaders – Reimagined** included. Turn it on in *Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs*.
- **Chunky** to pre-generate the server map.
- Defaults tuned for modest PCs: render distance **8** and simulation distance **6** on first launch (via Default Options, so your own settings are never overwritten on updates).

**A comfortable interface**
- **EMI** for recipes (with JEI integrations working), **Jade**, Mouse Tweaks, Inventory Essentials, Pick Up Notifier and TrashSlot.
- **Crafting Tweaks**, **Toast Control** (no more pop-up spam), **KeybindsPurger** (clears key conflicts) and **Clean Swing** (hit through tall grass).
- More in JEI/EMI: **JEI Stuff** (mob and chest loot, villager trades, bartering, fishing, plant drops and fluid interactions), **Just Enough Mekanism Multiblocks**, **Just Enough Professions** and **Just Enough Breeding**.
- **Just Zoom**: zoom key and a better spyglass.
- **Inventory Tweaks ReFoxed** (sort keys), **Tool Belt**, **Akashic Tome** (every guide book in one), **Fuel Goes Here** and **Accelerated Decay** (leaves fall fast).
- **Not Enough Animations**, **Better Advancements**, **Colorful Hearts** and **Overloaded Armor Bar** (one tidy row for extra hearts and armor), **More Overlays** (light level and chunk borders), **Smithing Template Viewer**, **Rebind Narrator** and **Bad Wither No Cookie** (no world-wide wither and dragon sounds).

## Quests

**120 quests in 12 chapters**, in English and Portuguese (they follow your game language). They are optional: a guide, not a gate. No mod is locked behind them.
Every quest gives XP, and almost all of them give items too: the big milestones give something for the next step (precision mechanisms, molecular assemblers, chemical tanks), and the smaller quests give a draw from the chapter's reward table (a stack of osmium, some silicon, an upgrade).

- **Welcome** and **First Steps**: commands, teams, voice chat, waystones, backpacks and Create.
- **Power: Getting Started**: which generator to build first (Furnator, water wheel + Alternator, Thermo Generator), batteries, cables and Flux Networks.
- **Mekanism**: double ores, then 3x, 4x and 5x, the hydrogen jetpack and the Digital Miner.
- **Storage** and **AE2: Autocrafting**: from drawers to your first ME network, Crafting CPU, patterns and the wireless terminal.
- **Create: Automation**, **Mystical Agriculture**, **Ars Nouveau**, **Voltaic Dynamics** and **Bubie**.

## Server

Download the **Server Pack** from the **Files** tab (additional file on each version). Client-only mods are already removed.

1. Extract the zip into a folder and install **Java 21**.
2. Run `start.sh` (Linux/macOS) or `start.bat` (Windows). On the first run, the script downloads and installs **NeoForge 21.1.265** by itself.
3. Accept the Minecraft EULA: open the generated `eula.txt`, change it to `eula=true` and run the script again.
4. Memory is set in `user_jvm_args.txt` (default: **8 GB**).
5. For voice chat, open **UDP port 24454** in your firewall.
6. Before opening to players, pre-generate the map: `/chunky radius 3000`, then `/chunky start`.
7. The first run creates `server.properties` with `view-distance=8` and `simulation-distance=6`. Raise them on a strong machine.

## Servidor

Baixe o **Server Pack** na aba **Files** (arquivo adicional de cada versão). Ele já vem sem os mods que só rodam no cliente.

1. Extraia o zip numa pasta e instale o **Java 21**.
2. Rode `start.sh` (Linux/macOS) ou `start.bat` (Windows). Na primeira vez, o script baixa e instala o **NeoForge 21.1.265** sozinho.
3. Aceite a EULA do Minecraft: abra o `eula.txt` criado, troque para `eula=true` e rode o script de novo.
4. A memória fica em `user_jvm_args.txt` (padrão: **8 GB**).
5. Para o chat de voz, libere a **porta UDP 24454** no firewall.
6. Antes de abrir para os jogadores, pré-gere o mapa: `/chunky radius 3000` e depois `/chunky start`.
7. A primeira execução cria o `server.properties` com `view-distance=8` e `simulation-distance=6`. Em máquina forte, aumente.
