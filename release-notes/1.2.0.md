## Bubie Pack - Tech, Magic & Robot Helpers 1.2.0

You can keep playing your 1.1.x world. New worlds start with the Program Chip progression; in an older world, turn on `advancedJobsNeedChip` in `serverconfig/bubie-server.toml` if you want it.

### Bubie 1.4.0
- **No corners to mark:** Shift + click a chest with the Clipboard and the Bubie works around it (radius set in its menu). The Quarry and the Digger dig in front of the chest.
- New **Guard** job: fights the monsters in its area with the weapon in its tool slot and keeps watch at night.
- **Program Chip**: in the pack, the Miner, Builder, Courier and Guard need it (it comes out of the Alloy Smelter).
- The **Station** menu lists the Bubies living there.
- **Español** in Bubie, Voltaic Dynamics (1.6.1) and Base Blueprint (1.3.1).

### Quests
- New: **Program Chip** and **Bubie the Guard**.
