## Bubie Pack 0.5.0

A version jump: the pack is getting close to 1.0. This version brings 37 mods from ATM10, including YUNG's structure overhauls. **Start a new world**: the new structures only appear in new chunks.

### Added (same versions as ATM10)
- **Structures:** YUNG's Better Mineshafts, Dungeons, Strongholds, Nether Fortresses, Desert Temples, Jungle Temples, Ocean Monuments, Witch Huts and End Island (with YUNG's API). They replace the vanilla structures without adding new ones.
- **Mobs:** Creeper Overhaul and Enderman Overhaul (variants that match their biome; they use Resourceful Config).
- **Create:** Create: Enchantment Industry (liquid experience, automatic enchanting and book copying).
- **Decoration:** Amendments, the Supplementaries companion (cauldron potion mixing, carpeted stairs, swinging lanterns and more).
- **Server:** Better Compatibility Checker. The server list shows whether your pack version matches the server's.
- **AE2:** Polymorphic Energistics (pick between conflicting recipes in the crafting and pattern encoding terminals).
- **Performance:** AI Improvements (lighter mob AI) and Sodium Extra (FPS counter, toggles for particles, animations, fog and more).
- **Shaders:** Euphoria Patches (extra options for the included Complementary shaders) and Iris Search (search the shader settings).
- **Comfort:** Inventory Tweaks ReFoxed (sort keys for your inventory and chests), Tool Belt, Akashic Tome (every guide book in one), Fuel Goes Here, Accelerated Decay (leaves fall fast), Comforts (sleeping bags and hammocks) and Cosmetic Armor Reworked.
- **Interface:** Not Enough Animations, Better Advancements, Colorful Hearts, Overloaded Armor Bar, More Overlays (light level and chunk borders), Smithing Template Viewer, Rebind Narrator and Bad Wither No Cookie (no world-wide wither and dragon sounds).
- **Tech:** Energy Meter (measure energy flow).
