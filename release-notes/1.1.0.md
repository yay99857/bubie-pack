## Bubie Pack - Tech, Magic & Robot Helpers 1.1.0

You can keep playing your 1.0.0 world.

### Bubie 1.2.0
- New **Courier** job: empties the chests, hoppers and furnace outputs in its area into its own chest. Feeding a Builder, it carries only what the blueprint still needs.
- Bubies in the same area **split the work**: each one claims the block (a lumberjack, the whole tree) it is on.
- A stuck Bubie (in a hole, behind a door or fence) teleports next to where it was going or shows **Stuck**, instead of standing still with no explanation.
- The Builder shows its **progress** (blocks placed / total) in the menu and on Jade, and its balloon says **which block it needs and how many**.

### Base Blueprint 1.3.0
- **Build with Bubie** button in the blueprint menu: your nearest Bubie builds the base where you are looking.

### Pack
- **Bubie chips** now come out of Voltaic's **Alloy Smelter** (2 galvanic alloy, 4 redstone and the chip's part).
- New **"Meet the Bubie"** quest in the first chapter, showing the way to it; new **Courier** quest.
- New name and logo: **Bubie Pack - Tech, Magic & Robot Helpers**.
