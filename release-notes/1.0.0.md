## Bubie Pack 1.0.0

You can keep playing your 0.6.x world: quest progress is kept.

### New: Bubie
- The third mod made for the pack: **Bubie**, the little robot from bubie.gg, as a helper. It follows you, carries items and, given a job and an area, works on its own and stores everything in a chest: **Lumberjack** (whole trees, replants), **Digger** (sand, gravel, dirt, clay), **Quarry** (with stairs, plugs lava and water), **Farmer** (Mystical Agriculture and Farmer's Delight included), **Miner** (veins or tunnels, `c:ores`) and **Collector**.
- **Bubie Clipboard** to mark the area, chest and home; **Bubie Station** that charges it with FE from any cable or coal; 6 upgrade **chips**.
- **Edit my Bubie**: casing, hoodie and screen-light colours, eyes and mouth from My Bubie on bubie.gg.
- Works with the rest of the pack: stores into Sophisticated Storage, Functional Storage, Create vaults and Mekanism bins; respects FTB Chunks claims (it breaks blocks as its owner); FTB Teams teammates can give it orders; Jade shows what it is doing.
- **Bubie the Builder (with Base Blueprint):** hand a Base Blueprint to a Bubie and it builds the base block by block, taking the blocks from its chest. When they run out it waits, and another Bubie (Lumberjack, Quarry) can keep filling the same chest.
- **Voltaic integration:** in the pack the Bubie is made with **voltaic ingots** and the Station with a **Basic Accumulator**; a **Pocket Accumulator** (or a Mekanism energy tablet) in the fuel slot charges the Bubie; the Miner can unload ore straight into the **Pulverizer**.

### Quests
- New **Bubie** chapter (9 quests, one for the Builder). The last one, "A friend for your Bubie", gives you **another Bubie**.
