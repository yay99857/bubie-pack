## Bubie Pack - Tech, Magic & Robot Helpers 1.2.1

You can keep playing your 1.2.0 world.

### Bubie 1.5.0
- **Runs on redstone or FE, not coal** (it is a robot): a redstone dust gives 5 minutes of work, a block 45. The Station takes redstone too, and the redstone a Bubie digs up tops up its fuel slot. Coal left in a fuel slot just stays there.
- **Disc slot in the DJ tab:** the disc no longer takes a storage slot or goes to the chest with the cargo, and taking it out while it plays stops the music.
- **DJ links:** Dropbox, Google Drive, GitHub and Audius share links play as they are. YouTube, Spotify and the like are refused with the reason, and a file from your computer explains how to share it.
- Says **"Its tool is out of energy"** when an electric tool (Atomic Disassembler, any FE tool) is empty.
- **Middle click** (Inventory Tweaks) no longer pulls the tool, fuel, chips and disc into the storage.
- Menu: tooltips on the buttons, the reason it has nothing to do, and the area shows in the world when you change the radius.

### Voltaic Dynamics 1.6.2
- Inventory Tweaks no longer mixes machine slots (inputs, upgrades, the accumulator's batteries).

### Quests
- The Bubie quest gives redstone instead of coal; Bubie and DJ texts updated.
