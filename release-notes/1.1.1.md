## Bubie Pack - Tech, Magic & Robot Helpers 1.1.1

You can keep playing your 1.1.0 world.

### Bubie 1.3.0: the DJ
- New **DJ** tab in the Bubie's menu: it plays the music discs in its storage (from any mod) and `.mp3`/`.ogg` links, for everyone nearby.
- Its screen becomes an equalizer, it dances and sends out notes, and the Bubies around it dance along.
- New **"Bubie the DJ"** quest in the Bubie chapter (reward: a music disc).
