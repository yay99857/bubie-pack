## Bubie Pack 0.6.0

Two mods made for Bubie Pack. You can keep playing your 0.5.x world: quest progress is kept.

### Added
- **Voltaic Dynamics 1.5.0:** compact FE tech. Combustion, Thermoelectric (heat next to cold) and Fluid (lava) Generators, Electric Furnace, Alloy Smelter and Pulverizer with Speed and Efficiency Upgrades, 1M and 8M FE Accumulators, Pocket Accumulator, Voltaic Cables, lag-free Item and Fluid Cables, Fluid Tank and Voltaic Wrench. Works with Mekanism, AE2 and Powah cables and machines; AlmostUnified unifies its dusts with the pack's.
- **Base Blueprint 1.1.2:** builds your first base in one click. Recipe: crafting table + 3 paper. Round, square, octagonal and hexagonal floors, walls with a door, a flat roof or a dome, a foundation on uneven ground, separate floor and wall materials, blocks from your inventory, backpack and nearby chests, and undo saved with the world.

### Quests
- New **Voltaic Dynamics** chapter with 16 quests, from the first Voltaic Ingot to the Advanced Accumulator.
- **Base Blueprint** quest in First Steps.
- 112 quests in 11 chapters now.
