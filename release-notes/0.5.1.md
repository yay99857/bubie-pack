## Bubie Pack 0.5.1

Quest rewards. You can keep playing your 0.5.0 world: quest progress is kept.

### Quests
- 34 quests now give, besides XP, a small item that helps with the next step without skipping it. For example: a Pickup Upgrade for the backpack quest, 2 Warp Scrolls for waystones, 2 Energy Tablets for the Energy Cube, 8 Silicon for the Inscriber, 8 Blank Patterns for patterns and 4 Source Gems for the spell book.
- Cheap things (dielectric paste, cables, drawers, belts, patterns) come as a copy; machines never do.
- 36 quests now give an item; the other 59 still give XP only.
