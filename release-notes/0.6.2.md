## Bubie Pack 0.6.2

You can keep playing your 0.6.x world: quest progress is kept (rewards already claimed do not change).

### Quests: bigger rewards
- More XP on every quest: 10 → 30, 25 → 60, 50 → 120 and 100 → 250.
- The 43 quests with an item give twice as much (up to a stack of 64): for example 32 coal, 16 silicon, 32 energy pipes, 128 cobblestone for the Base Blueprint. The first ME network quest gives a 4k component instead of 1k, and the iron quest also gives 32 torches. One-off upgrades (Pickup Upgrade, Stack Upgrade) and the bucket stay at 1.

### Base Blueprint 1.2.1
- The menu shows the base in **3D** with your materials: drag to turn it, scroll to zoom. The 2D/3D button switches to the pixel plan.
- "Walls" is now **"Wall height"** (the height of each storey). With more than one storey the height is at least 2; going from 1 to 2 storeys raises a lower height to 4.
- The in-world outline draws through its own buffer, a fix for it not showing with Sodium and Iris.
