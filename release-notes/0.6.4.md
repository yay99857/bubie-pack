## Bubie Pack 0.6.4

0.6.3 was rejected by CurseForge because **No Chat Reports** is no longer on CurseForge, so this version removes it and brings the 0.6.3 quest changes.
You can keep playing your 0.6.x world: quest progress is kept, quests you already completed let you claim their new reward, and No Chat Reports has no blocks or items.

### Removed
- No Chat Reports (the project is no longer on CurseForge).

### Quests: an item on almost every quest
Compared with ATM10, Craftoria and Enigmatica 10, which give an item on almost every quest. Before, 69 of the 112 quests gave only XP; now 10 do (the reading ones).
- **16 milestones now give a fixed item** for the next step, for example: 3 Precision Mechanisms, 2 Molecular Assemblers and 1 Pattern Provider, 2 pumps, 16 pipes and 1 Blaze Burner for the steam engine, 4 Refined Obsidian and 4 Reinforced Alloys for 5x dissolution.
- **43 smaller quests give a draw** from the chapter's reward table (Basics, Power, Mekanism, AE2, Create, Mystical, Ars Nouveau and Voltaic): a bundle of 1 to 32 items, such as 16 osmium ingots, 16 silicon or 32 inferium essence. Upgrades and diamonds come up less often.
- Metals come from All the Ores, the same ones Almost Unified uses in recipes. XP is unchanged.
