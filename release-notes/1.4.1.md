## Bubie Pack 1.4.1

Your 1.4.0 world carries over.

### Added
- **Just Reactors** 1.2.0, made for this pack: multiblock fission reactors (direct FE or steam), animated steam turbines, a heat exchanger with a closed coolant loop, a multiblock battery and the cyanite reprocessor.
  Works with Mekanism, AE2, Powah and any FE cable or fluid pipe; supports JEI/EMI, Jade and Carry On.
  Version 1.2.0 brings connected glass, automatic casing trim, a fission pulse on the fuel rods and a night glow on the turbine.

### Integration
- Fuel is **All the Ores uranium** (`c:ingots/uranium`); Just Reactors' own uranium ore is not generated, since ATO already generates it.
- **Almost Unified:** `justreactors` was added to the priorities after Mekanism. Extreme Reactors graphite, cyanite and blutonium now come out as Just Reactors items. The ones you already have keep working in recipes and in both mods' reactors.
- Furnace recipes coal/charcoal → graphite were disabled, like Extreme Reactors' (shift-click still sends coal to the fuel slot); graphite comes from the **blast furnace**.

### Heads-up: Extreme Reactors is leaving
- **Extreme Reactors** stays in the pack until the next major version (**2.0.0**) and will then be **removed**. Its reactors and turbines will disappear from the world along with whatever is inside them.
- You can switch now: build your new generators with **Just Reactors**. We will remind you again in the versions before the removal, with time to take your Extreme Reactors generators apart.
