## Bubie Pack - Tech, Magic & Robot Helpers 1.3.0

You can keep playing your 1.2.2 world: every Bubie gets a drive with everything it carried.

### Bubie 2.0.0
- **Hard Drives and SSDs:** what a Bubie carries lives on its **drive**, in a slot next to its fuel. Every Bubie comes with a **Hard Drive** (18 slots, its recipe now takes one); a **Solid-State Drive** gives it 36. Take the drive out and the cargo goes with it.
- The **Storage Chip is retired**: two of them and a diamond make a Solid-State Drive.
- **Name your Bubie** from its menu.
- **Makes its own way:** when no path leads where it must work, it digs down a pit, cuts steps, tunnels or bridges, then climbs out and fills the holes back in. Truly stuck, it shows a **?** on its screen.
- Hold **Alt + B** to see your Bubies through walls.

### Base Blueprint 1.4.0
- **"Built by: You / Bubie"** in the blueprint menu: with Bubie, right-click a block and your nearest Bubie builds the base there.

### Voltaic Dynamics 1.7.1
- Each cable shows what it carries on its core.

### Quests
- New **Hard Drives and SSDs** quest; the Bubie, chip, DJ and Station quests describe 2.0.0. Bubie quests give redstone instead of coal.
