## Bubie Pack 0.1.11

Less infinite loot: mining, ore processing and automation matter again. **Start a new world** with this version.

### Removed
- **Replication**. It copied any item from matter, which made factories and mining pointless. Titanium stays (Functional Storage and Industrial Foregoing need it).
- The Replication quest chapter (now 95 quests in 10 chapters).

### GeOre
- Geodes now only spawn for **coal, copper, iron and quartz** (Overworld and Nether). Diamond, emerald, gold, lapis, redstone and ancient debris no longer get geodes. Only affects newly generated chunks. GeOre Nouveau (Ars Nouveau source gem geodes) is unchanged.
