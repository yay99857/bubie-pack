## Bubie Pack 0.6.1

Updates the two mods made for the pack. You can keep playing your 0.6.0 world.

### Voltaic Dynamics 1.6.0
- Galvanic Alloy now comes only from the **Alloy Smelter**, like Mekanism's alloys come from the Metallurgic Infuser. Galvanic Dust is gone (hand recipe, furnace, blast furnace and Pulverizer): dusts smelt into ingots, alloys are made in a machine. Galvanic Dust already in a world disappears.
- The Galvanic Alloy quest now follows the Alloy Smelter quest.

### Base Blueprint 1.2.0
- **Storeys:** stack up to 16 floors, each as tall as the walls, with a floor between them and a hatch for a ladder. Build towers.
- Each blueprint builds **8 bases** (free in creative); repair it with paper in an anvil. Blueprints you already have get 8 uses too.
