## Bubie Pack 0.1.9

### Performance
- **Defaults for modest PCs**: render distance **8** and simulation distance **6** on first launch (vanilla uses 12 and 12).
  Set through **Default Options**, so they only apply to new installs. Already playing? Adjust them in *Options → Video Settings*.
- **Server**: the first run of `start.sh` / `start.bat` creates `server.properties` with `view-distance=8` and `simulation-distance=6`. An existing `server.properties` is left alone.

### Fixes
- **Shift-clicking coal or charcoal** with a furnace open now puts it in the fuel slot. Extreme Reactors' furnace recipes for graphite were sending it to the input slot instead. Graphite is still made in the **Blast Furnace**.

### Your settings stay yours
- Updates no longer reset your video settings (Sodium), shader choice (Iris), minimap and world map (Xaero) or FTB Chunks client options. The pack only sets them on the first launch.
