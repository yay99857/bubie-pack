## Bubie Pack 0.1.13

GeOre now matches ATM11, plus 28 light mods taken from ATM10: fixes, quality of life and integrations between the mods already here. None of them adds a new dimension, ore or big machine set. New geodes only appear in new chunks, so **start a new world** to get them all.

### GeOre
- Geodes spawn again for every vanilla ore, as in ATM11: coal, copper, iron, gold, diamond, emerald, lapis, redstone, quartz (Overworld and Nether) and ancient debris. This is GeOre's default config, the same one ATM11 ships.

### Removed
- **GeOre Nouveau**. ATM11 doesn't have it: its Dowsing Rods made geodes too easy to find and the GeOre Golem farmed them for you. Now you find geodes by exploring. GeOre Nouveau items already in a world disappear.

### Added (same versions as ATM10)
- **Server and friends:** Simple Backups (world backup every 2 hours, keeps the last 10, up to 25 GB, in the `simplebackups` folder), No Chat Reports and Crash Assistant (shows what crashed and which mod caused it).
- **Performance and fixes:** All The Leaks (memory leaks), Get It Together, Drops! (dropped items merge), Fast Workbench, NetherPortalFix and Model Gap Fix.
- **Interface:** Crafting Tweaks, Toast Control, KeybindsPurger, Clean Swing and Just Zoom (with Konkrete, which it needs).
- **JEI/EMI:** JEI Stuff (mob and chest loot, villager trades, bartering, fishing, plant drops and fluid interactions), Just Enough Mekanism Multiblocks, Just Enough Professions and Just Enough Breeding.
- **AE2:** MEGA Cells, ME Requester, AE2: Crafting Tree, AE2 Network Analyser and Applied Flux (FE in ME cells).
- **Ars Nouveau:** Ars Énergistique (Source in the ME network) and Ars Creo (Create compat).
- **Sophisticated:** Sophisticated Backpacks Create Integration, Sophisticated Storage Create Integration and Sophisticated Storage In Motion (storage minecarts and boats).
