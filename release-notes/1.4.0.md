## Bubie Pack 1.4.0

**Start a new world**: Terralith and L_Ender's Cataclysm change world generation, and several overpowered or redundant mods were removed to rebalance progression.

### Added
- **L_Ender's Cataclysm** (with Lionfish API): challenging endgame bosses, arenas and mythical gear to put your setup to the test.
- **Terralith**: new Overworld biomes and terrain built using vanilla blocks.
- **Handcrafted**: detailed furniture and interior decoration for your bases.
- **Carry On**: pick up and move chests, blocks and mobs with your hands.
- **GraveStone Mod**: clean gravestone on death (replaces Corail Tombstone).
- **Chat Heads** (client-only): shows player heads next to chat messages.

### Removed
- **Balance & progression:** Allthemodium, All The Tweaks, Grinding Ball Additions, BBL Strainers (+ In World Recipes & BBL Core), Botany Pots - Mystical Agriculture Compat, Growth Accelerator Tiers and Enchanting Infuser.
- **Redundancy & weight:** Agritech: Evolved, Animal Feeding Trough, XyCraft (Core, World, Machines, Override), Ender IO (+ Endergy) and Corail Tombstone.
- Cleaned obsolete mod configs from overrides and `xycraft_world` from Almost Unified priorities.

### Quests
- New **Final Goal** chapter (8 quests): hunt the Cataclysm bosses (Netherite Monstrosity, Ignis, The Harbinger, Maledictus), master the pack's six pillars and earn **Bubie Pack Master** (**133 quests in 13 chapters**).
- Updated quests for gravestones (GraveStone Mod), the alternative early generator (Voltaic Combustion Generator instead of Stirling Generator) and Mystical Agriculture farming (Bubie Farmer / Create / Industrial Foregoing).

### Fixes
- Window title is now set directly via KubeJS (`"Bubie Pack"`).
- Filtered client-only mods (`justzoom_`, `konkrete_`, `PickUpNotifier-`, `smithingtemplateviewer-`, `JustEnoughMekanismMultiblocks-`, `JustEnoughProfessions-`, `ae2ct-`, `chat_heads-`) out of the Server Pack.
- URL-encode mod filenames when downloading jars in `tools/build_server.py`.
